# AI SaaS Sales

Use this skill when preparing or improving a sales interaction for an AI-powered SaaS product.

## Workflow

- Identify the buyer, their use case, the current process, and the stage of the sale. State reasonable assumptions when details are missing.
- Connect product capabilities to the buyer's stated needs. Do not invent features, integrations, security claims, pricing, performance results, or return on investment.
- For discovery, focus questions on the workflow, business impact, data and privacy constraints, integrations, and buying process.
- For demos, show the smallest set of product workflows that prove the relevant value.
- For outreach, objection responses, and follow-ups, keep the message concise and end with a clear next step.
